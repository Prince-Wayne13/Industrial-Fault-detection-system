# PR-005

The system shall process at least one reading per second for every monitored parameter on every machine, and at least 40 readings per second in total (10 machines × 4 parameters). If a machine has extra parameters, the system shall still keep up, without breaching PR-001 or PR-002.

## How to test (draft)

- **Method:** measure
- **Seeded scenario:** Heavy load
- **What to do:** Count readings processed each second with 10 machines of 4 parameters.
- **Passes if:** At least 40 per second.
