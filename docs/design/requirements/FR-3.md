# FR-3

The system shall monitor backup power supply/UPS operating conditions, including internal temperature, charging/output current, and cooling fan speed.

## How to test (draft)

- **Method:** look
- **Seeded scenario:** Normal running
- **What to do:** Open the Overview and look at a backup power card.
- **Passes if:** Internal temperature, current and fan speed are all shown.

Status is one of: built, simulated, left-out, not-set. Change the `status` line above.
