# FR-10

All users shall be able to view fault history. Operators and Engineers shall be able to generate reports from fault history showing equipment performance and recurring faults.

## How to test (draft)

- **Method:** look
- **Seeded scenario:** Sustained warning
- **What to do:** Sign in as each role and open fault history. As an Operator or Engineer, generate a report.
- **Passes if:** Everyone can view history. Operator and Engineer can make a report showing performance and repeated faults.
