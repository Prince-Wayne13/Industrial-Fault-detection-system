# FR-22

The system shall keep a log of administrative actions (user account changes, equipment registration, threshold/configuration changes), recording who made the change and when.

## How to test (draft)

- **Method:** look
- **Seeded scenario:** Limit change
- **What to do:** Add a user, register a machine and change a limit, then open the change log.
- **Passes if:** Each change is listed with who and when.
