# FR-1

The system shall monitor motor operating conditions, including temperature, current, vibration, and rotational speed (RPM).

## How to test (draft)

- **Method:** look
- **Seeded scenario:** Normal running
- **What to do:** Open the Overview and look at a motor card.
- **Passes if:** Temperature, current, vibration and RPM are all shown.
