# PR-001a

When the status is critical, the system shall show the alert immediately after the status confirms it, and no later than 2 seconds afterwards. A single abnormal reading never raises an alert (FR-6).

## How to test (draft)

- **Method:** measure
- **Seeded scenario:** Sustained critical
- **What to do:** Time from the status confirming a critical fault to the alert on screen.
- **Passes if:** No more than 2 seconds.
