# FR-26

A user session shall expire after 5 minutes of inactivity, requiring re-authentication before further access. A user may set a shorter time, but not shorter than 1 minute and not longer than 5 minutes.

## How to test (draft)

- **Method:** look
- **Seeded scenario:** Login checks
- **What to do:** Set the sign-out time to 1 minute and leave the screen idle.
- **Passes if:** The person is signed out and must sign in again. 1 minute and 5 minutes are accepted, 6 is not.
