# PR-003

The system shall support at least 10 pieces of equipment monitored at the same time, across any combination of motors, water pumps, and backup power supplies, without breaching PR-001 or PR-002.

## How to test (draft)

- **Method:** measure
- **Seeded scenario:** Heavy load
- **What to do:** Run 10 machines at once and watch the update times.
- **Passes if:** Status and reading times stay within their limits.
