# FR-2

The system shall monitor water pump operating conditions, including bearing temperature, current draw, vibration, and rotational speed (RPM).

## How to test (draft)

- **Method:** look
- **Seeded scenario:** Normal running
- **What to do:** Open the Overview and look at a water pump card.
- **Passes if:** Bearing temperature, current, vibration and RPM are all shown.

Status is one of: built, simulated, left-out, not-set. Change the `status` line above.
