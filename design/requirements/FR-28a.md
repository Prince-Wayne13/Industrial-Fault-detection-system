# FR-28a

Passwords shall be at least 8 characters long, and the system shall accept passwords of 64 characters or more.

## How it is tested

- **Method:** look (draft)
- **Injection:** none
- **Check:** Try a 7-character password (refused) and a 64-character one (accepted).
