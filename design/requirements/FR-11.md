# FR-11

The system shall support both a near-range and a remote-range connection mode for accessing equipment data from the dashboard. The final demonstration shall provide a selectable simulated mode for each connection type.

## How it is tested

- **Method:** inject (draft)
- **Injection:** [[07-demo-control-panel|I-10]] Cut and restore connection
- **Check:** Switch between near and remote mode on the panel. The dashboard keeps working in each. In Auto mode, untick Bluetooth in range: the glow moves from the Bluetooth icon to the Wi-Fi icon and readings keep arriving. Tick it again: the glow moves back. Untick both: both icons go grey and show Offline.
- **Design note:** the icons and their rules are in [[top-bar]] and change 11 in [[srs-changes]].

Status is one of: built, simulated, left-out, not-set. Change the `status` line above.
