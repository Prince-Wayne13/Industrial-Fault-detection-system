# FR-16

The system shall not raise a duplicate alert for a fault condition that is already open and unacknowledged; it shall only raise a new alert once the condition has returned to normal and then reoccurs.

## How it is tested

- **Method:** inject (draft)
- **Injection:** [[07-demo-control-panel|I-06]] Hold and repeat
- **Check:** Hold the fault: no second alert. Clear it and trigger it again: a new alert.
