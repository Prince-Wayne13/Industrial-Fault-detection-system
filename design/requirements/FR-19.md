# FR-19

If a piece of equipment remains in maintenance mode beyond its stated duration, the system shall flag this as overdue on the dashboard.

- **Method:** inject (draft)
- **Injection:** [[07-demo-control-panel|I-14]] Fast-forward the clock
- **Check:** Fast-forward past the stated maintenance time. The "Overdue" mark appears.
