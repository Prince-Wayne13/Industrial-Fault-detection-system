# FR-26

A user session shall expire after 5 minutes of inactivity, requiring re-authentication before further access. A user may set a shorter time, but not shorter than 1 minute and not longer than 5 minutes.

## How it is tested

- **Method:** inject (draft)
- **Injection:** [[07-demo-control-panel|I-14]] Fast-forward the clock
- **Check:** Set a 1-minute time-out in Settings, try 0 and 6 (refused), then fast-forward the idle clock. The session ends.
