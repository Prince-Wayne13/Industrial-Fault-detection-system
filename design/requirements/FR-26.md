# FR-26

A user session shall expire after 5 minutes of inactivity, requiring re-authentication before further access. A user may set a shorter time, but not shorter than 1 minute and not longer than 5 minutes.

Status is one of: built, simulated, left-out, not-set. Change the `status` line above.
