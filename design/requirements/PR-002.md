# PR-002

The dashboard shall show a newly received sensor reading within 2 seconds. The 2 seconds is counted from the moment the processing component receives the reading to the moment it appears on the dashboard, and applies to displaying the reading itself; it does not include the FR-6 trend check that decides whether a warning or critical alert is raised (see PR-001).

Status is one of: built, simulated, left-out, not-set. Change the `status` line above.
