# FR-28b

The system shall not require a mix of capitals, numbers or symbols in a password, and shall not force password changes on a schedule.

## How it is tested

- **Method:** look (draft)
- **Injection:** none
- **Check:** Set a password with no capitals, numbers or symbols. It is accepted. No change is forced.
