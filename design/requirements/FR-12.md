# FR-12

The system shall continue collecting, evaluating, and displaying locally available equipment status if the connection between system components is temporarily interrupted, and shall store any data collected during the interruption for transmission once the connection is restored.

Status is one of: built, simulated, left-out, not-set. Change the `status` line above.
