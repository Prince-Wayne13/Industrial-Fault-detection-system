# FR-10

All users shall be able to view fault history. Operators and Engineers shall be able to generate reports from fault history showing equipment performance and recurring faults.

## How it is tested

- **Method:** look (draft)
- **Injection:** none
- **Check:** Open the history on a machine page. Generate a report as Operator or Engineer.
